# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

prompt-fixer is a set of written instructions for Claude. It has no code, no servers, no MCP connectors and no analytics.

## What it handles

The skill reads the draft prompt you type and your answers to its questions, inside your own Claude session. It does not read your project files, connectors or any other data on its own.

## What it stores or sends

Nothing. The plugin does not store, log or send any data anywhere. Its author never receives your prompts or anything else from your session.

If you paste an API key, token or password into a prompt, the skill is told to leave it out of the rewritten prompt and tell you it was removed.

Your conversations are handled by Claude itself, under Anthropic's own privacy policy.
